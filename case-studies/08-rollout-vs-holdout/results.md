## The launch decision

Each design ships when the estimate it can produce for the longest horizon is significantly positive. That is the long-run ITT when there is a holdout and the short-run ITT otherwise. The right call is to ship when the true long-run ITT is meaningfully positive, and not to ship under no effect or a novelty effect that fades to near zero (true long-run ITT +0.0045).
